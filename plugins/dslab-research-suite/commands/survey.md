---
description: Survey an arxiv paper into an Obsidian note
argument-hint: [arxiv-url-or-id]
---

Use the arxiv MCP server to fetch the paper at $ARGUMENTS. Then invoke the survey-paper skill to generate a structured Obsidian survey note with:
1. Paper Digest style summary (why this research matters)
2. Iterative Writing principles-based detailed analysis
3. Flow diagram (Mermaid)

Save the output as an Obsidian-compatible markdown file.
