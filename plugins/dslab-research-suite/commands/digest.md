Use the arxiv MCP server to fetch the paper at $ARGUMENTS. Then invoke the paper-digest skill to produce a concise, insight-centered summary suitable for sharing on Discord, Slack, or Twitter.

Focus on WHY the research matters, not just what it does. Output a single-paragraph digest.
